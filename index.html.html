    What This Is & Why I Made It

    Most CISSP practice tools quiz raw technical trivia—yet the real exam tests whether you can think like a security manager while navigating dense wording, double negatives, strict chronology, and competing business constraints. I built InfoSec Exam Studio from scratch as an independent, offline-capable simulator to practice both halves of the challenge: dissecting tricky wording in a calm lo-fi study space, and proving readiness under authentic timed Computerized Adaptive Testing (CAT) pressure.
